# The man in the glass: where the film came from

`someone.mp4` is the man who appears in the dark screen once the set has been switched off (tv.tsx). He is nobody
real: no photograph or recording of a person was used.

| Step | What was made | Made with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The still | a man standing in a dark room, from a text description, then the same picture with everything but him made black | Gemini image generation (Gemini Nano Banana 2.1), through OpenCode on Anomaly's account |
| The movement | eight seconds from that still as the first frame: he blinks, tilts his head, takes a step nearer | MiniMax H3, through Higgsfield, on an Anomaly team member's account |
| The file | scaled to 640 x 480, its levels raised (it is kept brighter than it is shown), encoded as H.264 without sound | ffmpeg |

Terms: each service's own terms of use govern what it generates; the film is in the repository for this page on
those terms.

The set's sounds have their own note (`sound/SOURCES.md`), and the commercial's sound and fonts theirs
(`commercial/SOURCES.md`).
